SELECT
    n.nspname AS schema_name,
    c.relname AS table_name,
    p.attrnums AS dist_cols,
    pg_get_indexdef(i.indexrelid) AS dist_expr
FROM gp_distribution_policy p
JOIN pg_class c ON p.localoid = c.oid
JOIN pg_namespace n ON c.relnamespace = n.oid
LEFT JOIN pg_index i ON (i.indrelid = c.oid AND i.indnatts = array_length(p.attrnums, 1))
WHERE (n.nspname, c.relname) IN (
    ('dm_calc','storage_sales_bundles_amount'),
    ('dict_dds','counterparty'),
    ('dict_dds','address'),
    ('dict_dds','material_specification'),
    ('dict_dds','material_shape_texts'),
    ('dict_dds','transport_hub_texts'),
    ('dict_dds','transportation_stage_texts'),
    ('dict_dds','country_texts')
);
Если fact = RANDOM → это главная потенциальная причина.
